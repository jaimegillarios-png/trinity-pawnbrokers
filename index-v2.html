How a Trinity pawn loan works

Five steps. Your item back at the end.

A pawn loan is the oldest, simplest borrowing there is: your item is the security, so there are no credit checks and nothing touches your credit file. Plainly, because it matters: if the loan isn’t repaid or renewed at the end of its 6-month term, your item may be sold to settle it — you owe nothing further.
